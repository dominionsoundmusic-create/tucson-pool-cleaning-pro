Or send a short request

Rather not call right now? Fill in the form and your request goes to an independent local pool service company, which calls you back. The call is still the quickest way in at {{ site.business.phone_display }}, any hour.

Your details go only to the independent local pool company that will call you back. They are not sold. See the privacy policy.

What to say when the assistant answers

The assistant only needs a quick picture of the problem. A few plain details let the pool company know what it is walking into before it says hello.

  • What you are seeing: green or cloudy water, a pump that hums or stays silent, a water level that keeps dropping, white crust on the tile, a light that will not turn on.
  • When it started, and anything that happened just before, such as a monsoon storm, a power outage or a party.
  • Where the pool is: the town or neighborhood, for example Oro Valley, Rita Ranch, Sahuarita or near Tanque Verde, and a nearby cross street if you like.
  • Whether you are after regular visits, a single cleanup or a fix.
  • The best number for a call back, and good times to reach you.

You do not need to know the name of a part or what is wrong. Describing what you see and hear is enough.

If the pool company cannot pick up

Sometimes the company is on another call or out at a pool. In that case the assistant notes your name, callback number and area, plus a short note on what the pool is doing, and hands all of that over for a return call. The line cannot tell you exactly when that will happen. Keep your phone nearby and answer unfamiliar numbers for a while, because the call back may not come from {{ site.business.phone_display }}.

Once you are talking with the company, everything from there is between the two of you: the visit, the diagnosis, the quote, the work and the bill. Before repair or equipment work, get the company's license number and search it in the Registrar of Contractors' public license lookup. Nobody at the line checks that for you.

Call someone else first in an emergency

Smell gas at the pool heater

Leave the area at once and keep others away. Do not touch the heater, switches or anything that could spark. Once you are clear, dial 911, then the Southwest Gas leak line at 877-860-6020.

Someone is hurt or in the water

Call 911 right away. A pool line is never the right number for a medical emergency or a child in trouble in the water.

Sparks, shocks or a burning smell

Keep everyone out of the water and away from the equipment. If you can reach the breaker safely, switch the pool circuit off. Electrical repairs are work for a professional.

Everything else pool related

For problems that are not an emergency, call the line. A green pool, a stopped pump or a slow leak can wait the few minutes it takes to reach a local company.

What happens to the details you give

The automated assistant may record or log what you say on the call, for example your name and number, your part of town and the trouble you describe, so that it can be handed to the pool business that takes your call. If you send the request form instead, the same kind of details are passed on the same way, so a local company can call you back. The privacy policy explains how the line and this website are designed to handle information, and the terms of use set out what the line is and is not responsible for.

The line can be paid a referral fee by the companies. That never changes the fact that the call itself is free to you.

Where the line takes calls from

Call from anywhere in Tucson and the nearby communities of Pima County: the city itself, from the east side out toward Rita Ranch, plus Oro Valley, the Catalina Foothills, Marana, Vail, Sahuarita and Green Valley. Each town page covers local water, weather and rules worth knowing.
