Safety

Pool chemicals must never be combined. Keep each one in its original container in a dry, locked place that children cannot reach, and use it only as its label directs. Work on pool wiring, lights, motors or gas lines belongs with qualified professionals. A gas smell near a pool heater means get away from it first, then phone 911 and the Southwest Gas leak line once you are clear. Keep children away from any unfenced or uncovered pool, and never leave a child alone near water.
