{%- endmacro %} {% macro license_check() -%}

Look up the contractor license. Arizona's Registrar of Contractors (ROC) licenses contractors. Its R-6 license, Swimming Pool Service and Repair, covers servicing and minor repairs on home pools, while bigger jobs such as building a pool or replacing the whole plaster or pebble interior fall under pool construction licenses such as B-5. The ROC does not spell out whether cleaning alone needs a license, so ask the company which license it holds. Get the ROC number and search it on the ROC contractor search (opens in a new tab), which shows whether the license is active, its classification and recent complaints. The line does not check licenses for you.
