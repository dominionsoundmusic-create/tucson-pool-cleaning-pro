About Tucson Pool Cleaning Pro

Local Technicians, Local Water

Tucson Pool Cleaning Pro is a residential pool service company covering the Tucson metro — central Tucson, Oro Valley, Marana, Catalina Foothills, Sahuarita, and Vail. A good pool service handles weekly cleaning, water chemistry, equipment repair, and tile restoration for pools running in some of the hardest water in Arizona.

We are not a franchise and we are not a scheduling app that sends a different person every week. The same technician services your pool every visit, knows your equipment, knows your water, and notices when something changes before it becomes a repair bill. That consistency is the difference between a pool that stays clean and one that surprises you.

What Gets Done

Weekly pool cleaning — skimming, brushing, vacuuming, chemistry testing and balancing, filter and equipment checks. Ask what the monthly price includes.

Equipment repair — pumps, motors, filters, heaters, salt cells, lights, and plumbing leaks. Diagnosed on site, priced before work starts, fixed the same visit when parts allow.

Tile cleaning — calcium scale removal by media blasting. No drain, no damage to the tile glaze, swimmable the same day.

One-time deep cleans — for pools that have been sitting, just purchased, or reopening after the winter. Full chemistry reset, filter teardown, and a written report of what needs attention.

Why Tucson Is Different

Tucson tap water runs around 220 parts per million calcium hardness. That is above what every major equipment manufacturer recommends, and it means every pool here deposits calcium on heaters, salt cells, tile, and pump internals from the day it fills. A good Tucson pool service calibrates its chemistry, equipment checks and maintenance intervals for hard desert water, not for national averages.

Tucson pools also deal with monsoon season, 300-plus days of UV that eats through chlorine, and palo verde debris that loads skimmer baskets faster than pool owners expect. If you have had pool service before and it felt like they were just skimming the surface and leaving, that is probably exactly what happened. A good pool service does not work that way.
