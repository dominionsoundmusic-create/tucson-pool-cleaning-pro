Tucson Pools Run in Hard Water and Hard Sun

Every pool in the Tucson metro fills with water that runs around 220 parts per million calcium hardness — above what pool equipment manufacturers recommend. That calcium deposits on every surface the water touches: heater cores, salt cell plates, pump internals, filter grids, and the waterline tile. Add 300-plus days of direct UV that burns through chlorine faster than in any mild climate, and you have a pool that needs weekly attention to stay swimmable and monthly attention to stay out of the repair shop.

That is what we do. We run weekly routes across Tucson, Oro Valley, Marana, the Catalina Foothills, Sahuarita, and Vail. Every visit follows the same checklist — skim, brush, vacuum, test, adjust, inspect — so nothing gets skipped. When something breaks, we diagnose and fix it on the same visit when parts allow. When the tile line crusts over with calcium, we blast it clean without damaging the glaze.

Questions

What areas do you serve?

We service pools across the Tucson metro — central Tucson, Oro Valley, Marana, Catalina Foothills, Sahuarita, Vail, and Catalina. If you are in Pima County and you have a pool, we can reach you.

What is included in weekly pool service?

Every weekly visit includes skimming, brushing walls and tile, vacuuming, emptying baskets, testing and adjusting water chemistry (chlorine, pH, alkalinity, cyanuric acid, calcium hardness), checking filter pressure, and inspecting equipment. All routine chemicals are included in the monthly price.

Do you handle repairs or just cleaning?

Both. We repair pumps, filters, heaters, salt cells, lights, and plumbing leaks. Most cleaning customers call us first when something breaks because we already know their pool. Repairs are quoted separately from the monthly service price.

Why does pool equipment wear out faster in Tucson?

Tucson tap water runs around 220 parts per million calcium hardness — above what equipment manufacturers recommend. That calcium deposits on heater cores, salt cells, pump internals, and tile. Combined with extreme UV and 300-plus days of sun, equipment here takes more punishment than the same hardware in a milder climate.

How much does weekly pool service cost?

Monthly pricing depends on pool size, whether you have a spa, and whether you run chlorine or salt. Call with your address and pool size and we will give you a flat monthly price over the phone. Chemicals are included. There are no per-visit charges.
